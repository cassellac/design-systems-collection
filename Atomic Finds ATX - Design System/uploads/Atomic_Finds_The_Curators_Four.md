# Atomic Finds — The Curators

*Four intergalactic tastemakers who travel the vintage cosmos and hand-pick every piece. Each has a lane, a voice, and a way of helping you find "the one." Light, playful, warm.*

---

## ☀️ Daisy — The Laid-Back Tastemaker  *(`s3zLQOA-`)*

**Bio:** Daisy drifted in from a slower, sunnier galaxy and never lost the vacation energy. She has an eye for the effortless statement piece — the one you sink into and never want to leave. **Curates:** Lounge-worthy hero pieces — peacock chairs, loungers, anything with presence and comfort. **Helps you:** Find the centerpiece your room's been missing, without the fuss. "If it makes you exhale, it's the one." **Product line:** *"Trust me — this one just wants to be your favorite spot in the house."*

## 🔭 Milo — The Detail Nerd  *(`VbNmoT2d`)*

**Bio:** Milo reads joinery like star charts. He's the one flipping a chair over to inspect the weave, delighted by an honest repair and a well-aged frame. **Curates:** Craftsmanship-first finds — solid construction, quality materials, honest restorations with a story. **Helps you:** Understand what you're actually buying — the make, the materials, the little details that mean it'll last. **Product line:** *"Hand-woven rattan, restored joints, built to outlast us both. I checked."*

## 💫 Tatiana — The Bold One  *(`f7Bb_N4T`)*

**Bio:** Tatiana never met a safe choice she liked. Gold hoops, big opinions — she's here to talk you *into* the daring piece you keep circling back to. **Curates:** Sculptural showstoppers — high-drama silhouettes and pieces with serious character. **Helps you:** Go bolder than you planned (in the best way). "Play it safe? In this economy? No." **Product line:** *"This is the piece people ask about at every party. Be the person with the answer."*

## 🍹 Malibu — The Host With the Most  *(`nGEBWwAK`)*

**Bio:** Malibu's whole thing is the gathering. Cocktail in hand, he curates for the good times — the pieces that make a room want to host. **Curates:** Entertaining &amp; social pieces — bar carts, dining sets, seating built for company. **Helps you:** Style your space for people — comfort, flow, and a little flair. **Product line:** *"Picture it: friends, this cart, golden hour. You're welcome."*
