2. Legal Guardrails & Liability Shield

Critical Legal
⚠️ Crucial Legal Distinction: We must maintain a sharp, documented line between educational personal growth and medical practice / certified training. Read the full step-by-step document here: Legal Steps to Market & Package Medical Expertise Online ➔
  • Mandatory Universal Disclaimers
    Embed in video intros, course dashboards, worksheets, and website footers: "For educational and informational purposes only. Does not constitute medical advice or certified professional training."
  • Explicit "No Doctor-Patient / Clinician Relationship" Clause
    Enrollment and video consumption never establish a professional-client or doctor-patient relationship.
  • Strict Certificate Phrasing Standards
    Certificates must read: "Certificate of Completion: Awarded for personal growth and educational fulfillment in [Course Name]." Never use "CEU", "CME", or "Certified Specialist" without official state board accreditation.
  • Business Entity & Asset Protection
    Form dedicated LLC or corporate entity to shield personal assets from operational liability.
  • Privacy Policy & Terms of Service
    GDPR/CCPA compliant handling of user accounts, payments, and student progress data.

3. Video Production Strategy: Headless vs. Avatars

Creative Strategy

Explore the spectrum of video delivery. We can achieve extreme visual polish without requiring Mom to spend hours filming on camera.

Option A: Pure Faceless / Headless Dynamic animated slides, clinical flowcharts, annotated case studies, and voiceover audio. 100% focused on clarity.
Option B: AI Digital Avatar A photorealistic AI presenter delivers scripted lessons. High visual polish, consistent presence, no cameras needed.
Option C: Voice-Synced Avatar Clone Mom's natural voice (or record voiceover) and map it to the digital avatar. Maximum personal authority without filming.
Option D: Hybrid Approach Short personal audio/video intro from Mom, followed by faceless diagram walkthroughs for technical learning.
  • Review Comfort Level & Time Commitment
    Does Mom want to record voiceover herself, or review and approve AI synthesized voiceover?
  • Produce 2-Minute Proof-of-Concept Pilot
    Anthony will produce Lesson 1 in both Faceless Slides and Voice-Synced Avatar for side-by-side evaluation.
