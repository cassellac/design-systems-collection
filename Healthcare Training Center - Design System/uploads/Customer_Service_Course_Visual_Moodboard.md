## Direction Notes
- **Illustration track** shares consistent line weight, palette, and background treatment for cohesion across a multi-section email
- **Photoreal track** offers a contrast option — warmer, more human feel if the client wants photographic credibility
- Both tracks use the Customer Service accent sky blue **#3B7DD8** as the primary color signal
- Any of the 16:9 images work as top-of-email heroes; 1:1 tiles pair well as mid-email section breaks
