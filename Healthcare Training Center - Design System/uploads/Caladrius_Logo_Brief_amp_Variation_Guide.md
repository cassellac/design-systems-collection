# Healthcare Training Center — Logo Brief &amp; Variation Guide

---

## The Symbol Behind the Mark

In Roman and Medieval mythology, the healing bird was a snow-white bird kept in the courts of kings — rare, valuable, and believed to hold the power of life itself. When brought to the bedside of a sick person, it made a choice: if it turned away, the patient would not recover. But if it looked toward the patient, it would draw the illness out of their body and into itself, then fly upward to the sun — burning the sickness away completely. The bird symbolizes **selfless care, accurate diagnosis, and the willingness to bear another's burden** in the act of healing. For an organization training the next generation of healthcare workers in utmost respect and care, there is no more fitting symbol.

---

## Why This Symbol Maps to Healthcare Training

The bird does not merely treat — it sees, decides, and acts. That mirrors the clinical and human skills this training center builds: careful observation, sound judgment, compassionate action. The bird's upward gaze and flight toward the sun give the mark a quality of aspiration and purpose that no cross or caduceus can replicate.

## The 5 Active Icon Variations

*(Note: Prior text-bearing wordmark lockups have been moved to `assets/brand/logos/_archive/` in favor of clean icon marks and dynamic HTML/CSS typographic lockups).*

**1. Compound / Negative Bird — Navy Circle (`logo-compound-navy.png`)** The bird is carved out of a solid navy circle as negative space — cream revealed through the silhouette. A classical compound mark with heritage weight. *Use: certificate seals, embossed letterhead, dark-background stamps.*

**2. Compound / Negative Bird — Teal Circle (`logo-compound-teal.png`)** The same compound treatment in teal, adding warmth and approachability. *Use: lanyards, social media profile icons, collateral where the brand's secondary color leads.*

**3. Sun Halo — Amber Arc (`logo-halo-amber.png`)** A thin amber arc crowns the bird's head like a halo, evoking the mythological moment of the bird about to ascend to the sun. Reverent and distinct. *Use: diplomas, certificates of completion, awards.*

**4. Icon Only — Sun + Teal (`logo-icon-sun.png`)** The sun-behind-upper mark stripped of text. Square, balanced, favicon-ready. *Use: app icons, favicons, social profile photos, embroidery.*

**5. Icon Only — Compound Navy Circle (`logo-icon-compound.png`)** The compound bird knockout mark with no text. Maximum contrast and versatility. *Use: favicons, app icons, wax seals, embossed stamps.*

## Usage Guidance

**Clear space:** Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the width of the "C" in the wordmark on all sides.

**Minimum size:** Wordmark lockups no smaller than 120px / 1 inch wide. Icon-only marks no smaller than 32px.

**Color rules:**

- **Navy #1E3A6E** — primary. Always first choice for text and silhouettes.
- **Teal #2B8FA9** — secondary. Used for the sun mark and teal circle variant.
- **Cream #FAF7F2** — surface. Default background. Also used as the knockout color in compound marks.
- **Amber #C9973A** — accent only (V5 halo). Do not use as a primary surface color.

**Do:** Use the compound navy mark reversed on dark backgrounds. Use icon-only marks at small sizes where wordmarks become illegible.

**Don't:** Recolor the bird silhouette. Distort or rotate the mark. Place wordmark text over the icon. Use the teal circle on a teal background.
