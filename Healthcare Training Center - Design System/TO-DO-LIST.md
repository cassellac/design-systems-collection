## 2. Legal Guardrails & Business Structuring
> **Crucial Rule:** Draw a strict, non-negotiable boundary between **educational content** and **medical practice/certified clinical licensing**.  
> *Detailed legal guidance is documented in [legal-steps-to-market-packag-medical-expertise-online.md](file:///Users/cuus/Downloads/Design%20Systems/Healthcare%20Training%20Center%20-%20Design%20System/legal-steps-to-market-packag-medical-expertise-online.md).*

- [ ] **Mandatory Disclaimers on All Touchpoints:**
  - Add explicit disclaimer to all video intros/outros, course materials, PDFs, and website footers:
    > *"This content is for educational and informational purposes only. It does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or certified professional training."*
- [ ] **No Doctor-Patient / Clinician-Client Relationship:**
  - Clearly state that enrollment, viewing videos, or downloading templates does not create any clinician-patient or consulting relationship.
- [ ] **Certificate Framing (Strict Language Compliance):**
  - Use: *"Certificate of Completion: Awarded for personal growth and educational fulfillment in [Course Name]."*
  - **Never use:** "Certified Medical Training," "CEU," "CME," "Licensed," or "Clinical Credential" unless formally accredited by regulatory state boards.
- [ ] **Business Entity Formation:**
  - Establish a formal LLC or corporate entity to protect personal assets from liability.
  - Set up dedicated business banking and liability insurance if advisable.
- [ ] **Terms of Service & Privacy Policy:**
  - Draft GDPR/CCPA-compliant privacy policy for collecting student names, emails, and payments.

## 3. Video Production Strategy: Headless vs. Avatars vs. Live
*Discuss comfort level, authenticity, time commitment, and technical execution.*

- [ ] **Evaluate Production Models:**
  - **Option A — Pure Faceless / Headless Video:**
    - High-quality presentation slides, motion graphics, screen captures, case studies, and clinical diagram overlays with clean voiceover.
    - *Pros:* Zero camera anxiety, fast revisions, professional and focused on learning material.
  - **Option B — AI-Generated Digital Avatar (e.g., HeyGen, Synthesia):**
    - A consistent photorealistic presenter avatar delivers scripted lessons.
    - *Pros:* Scalable, no recording sessions required, highly polished visual delivery.
  - **Option C — Voice-Synced Avatar (Mom's Voice + Digital Avatar):**
    - Clone Mom's natural voice (via ElevenLabs with consent) or record real voiceover, synced to the digital presenter.
    - *Pros:* Personal warmth and authentic authority without needing to be on camera.
  - **Option D — Hybrid Presentation:**
    - Short live-video greeting or audio intro from Mom, followed by structured faceless slide and workflow instruction.
- [ ] **Decision & Next Action:**
  - Select primary pilot format for Module 1.
  - Test a 2-minute sample script with both faceless slides and voice-synced avatar to compare.
